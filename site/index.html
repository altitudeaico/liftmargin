Intake brief

Reported condition

Customer reports vibration on motor M-204 after an earlier bearing repair. No current measurement report was supplied.

Candidate repair history

A service report records a drive-end bearing replacement and completed test on M-204B in March. Its connection to this enquiry is unconfirmed. It does not establish current condition.

Questions for the reviewer

  • Check whether a serial number or documented cross-reference can establish the asset match.
  • Decide whether current vibration measurements are needed at this stage; none were supplied.

Review is an acknowledgement, not job approval.

    PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

    Before we begin.

    Does this replace our job management system?

    The proposed workflow is designed around the records and systems your team uses. The pilot establishes which imports, access and integrations are practical.

    Does AI make engineering decisions?

    No. The service organises and references information for review. Your team retains technical judgement, repair decisions and approval.

    What happens to our documents?

    Access, storage, retention and deletion arrangements must be agreed before company records are accepted. Please do not send confidential records in an initial enquiry.

    What does the pilot cost?

    The fee is agreed after the workflow and test scope are understood. The initial conversation is free and carries no obligation.

    Can you guarantee time savings?

    No. The pilot measures whether the workflow improves your handover. The fictional demonstration is not evidence of customer savings or operational results.
